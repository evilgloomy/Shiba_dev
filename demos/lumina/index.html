ABOUT LUMINA

A clinic built for unhurried care

Separate from our exam suite — this is the story of who we are, where we practise, and how we protect your information.

Our story

LUMINA began as a fictional partnership between clinicians tired of 12-minute slots and patients who travel between Hong Kong and Vancouver. The concept: one care team, two calm rooms, and records that follow you — not the other way around.

We design visits around conversation first, diagnostics second, and paperwork last. Every membership narrative on this demo site is illustrative only.

Hours & locations

  • Hong Kong · Admiralty — Mon–Fri 08:30–18:00 · Sat by appointment
  • Vancouver · Yaletown — Mon–Fri 09:00–17:30 · Virtual bridging visits
  • Concierge line — Same-day triage for members (demo fiction)

Privacy note

Health information on a real LUMINA site would be protected under applicable privacy law. This concept demo stores form data only in your browser — nothing is transmitted to a clinic server. Do not enter real medical details.
