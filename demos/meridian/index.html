Suites

Rooms composed for long stays, not layovers.

Three fictional room types. Availability is handled by the house desk — nothing on this page is a public rate.

Dining

A dining room, and a tea library.

Menus change with the market on each shore. Dietary notes stay on the guest profile inside the house desk.

The North Room

Evening service only. A short wood-fired menu and a harbour table kept for in-house guests.

  • First seating and second seating
  • In-room course for the suites, on request
  • Children’s plates without a separate menu

Tea Library

Morning through late afternoon. Oolong, smoked black, and a quiet pastry case. Laptops are welcome; calls are not.

  • Breakfast baskets to the room before 10:00
  • Allergen notes passed to the kitchen, not shouted
  • A low table reserved for working guests
