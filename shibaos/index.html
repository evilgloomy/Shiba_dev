The product idea產品核心

The interface is simple.
The system underneath is not.介面可以很簡單
背後系統並不簡單

ShibaOS hides orchestration complexity behind one operating surface. People interact with a company, not with a collection of AI tools.

ShibaOS 把複雜的協調、模型、工具與工作流程隱藏在一個營運介面之後。使用者面對的是「公司」，而不是一堆 AI 工具。

01

A persistent company brain持續存在的公司大腦

ShibaOS keeps durable context around people, projects, policies, decisions, assets, and operating history.

持續保存人物、項目、政策、決策、資產與營運歷史，讓公司知識不會隨一次對話消失。

02

A human interface to AI work自然的人類操作介面

Talk to Mina, type a request, or use the command center. The interaction stays human even when many systems work underneath.

可以直接跟 Mina 說話、打字或使用 Command Center。即使背後有多個系統協作，操作仍然自然。

03

Agents that coordinate work真正協調工作的 Agents

Investigate across data sources, delegate bounded tasks, compare evidence, prepare actions, and verify outputs.

跨資料源調查、分派受控任務、比較證據、準備行動並驗證結果。

04

Control stays with the company控制權留在公司手上

Sensitive actions can require approval. Workloads can run locally, on owned GPUs, or through approved cloud services.

敏感操作可要求人工審批，工作負載亦可在本地、自有 GPU 或核准的雲端服務上運行。

ShibaOS 的最終工作區會把公司狀態、即時營運、對話、調查、工作流程與審批放在同一個介面。

Architecture架構

One experience, layered underneath.一個體驗，背後由多層系統支撐

The product surface stays simple while ShibaOS routes context, reasoning, tools, voice, workflows, and compute behind the scenes.

使用者只看到簡單介面，ShibaOS 在背後處理背景資料、推理、工具、語音、工作流程與算力路由。

ContextCompany Brain

WorkAgent Runtime

Objectives, bounded delegation, evidence and verification.

SystemsTools + Integrations

Email, docs, chat, databases, APIs and business software.

ComputeShiba Compute

Local GPUs, workers, private infrastructure and approved cloud.

Deployment model部署模式

Cloud convenience without giving up infrastructure choice.保留雲端便利，同時保留基建選擇權

Different companies have different privacy, latency, cost, and control requirements. ShibaOS is designed around flexible execution rather than a single mandatory cloud.

不同公司對私隱、延遲、成本與控制權有不同要求。ShibaOS 的方向是彈性執行，而不是強迫所有工作都走同一個雲端。
